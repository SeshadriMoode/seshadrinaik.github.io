About Me

I hold a PhD in Civil Engineering from Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya – BarcelonaTech (BIT research group), at the intersection of traffic modelling, control, and automated vehicles.

My work focuses on how automated and human-driven vehicles interact, combining theory with simulation to understand system-level behavior.
